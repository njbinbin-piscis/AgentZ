# Code of Conduct

**English** | [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

AgentZ adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. Translations, including [Chinese](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/zh-cn/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), are available on the Contributor Covenant site.

## Our pledge, in short

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Expected behaviour includes being respectful of differing viewpoints, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information without permission, and sexualized language or imagery.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and other channels) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers privately through the repository's [private reporting form](https://github.com/njbinbin-piscis/AgentZ/security/advisories/new) (mark it as a conduct report) or by contacting a maintainer directly on GitHub. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and temporarily or permanently ban contributors, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
