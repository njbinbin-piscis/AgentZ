Overview
This guide covers working offline. It explains the setup and the core steps needed to operate without a network connection.

Backup instructions
1. Identify the files and settings you need to keep.
2. Choose a backup destination, such as an external drive or a local folder.
3. Copy the selected items to that destination.
4. Verify the copies open correctly.
5. Repeat the backup on a regular schedule.
